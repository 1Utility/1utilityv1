Frequently Asked Questions

Why can't I withdraw 100% of my NPS corpus at retirement?
NPS is structured specifically as a pension product — current rules mandate that at least 40% goes into an annuity to generate ongoing income, with the rest available as a lump sum withdrawal.
Is the NPS return guaranteed?
No — NPS returns depend on the market performance of the equity/debt/government-bond mix you choose within your NPS account, so actual returns will vary from any assumed figure.
How is NPS different from EPF?
EPF is mandatory for many salaried employees with employer matching; NPS is a voluntary (or in some cases mandatory for government employees) market-linked pension scheme with more investment choice but also more market risk.
