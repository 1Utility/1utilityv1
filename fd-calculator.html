FD Formula

Maturity Amount = P × (1 + r/n)(n×t)

P = Principal
r = Annual Rate
n = Compounding frequency per year
t = Time in years

Example

₹5,00,000 @ 7.1% for 3 years (Quarterly compounding)
Maturity: ₹6,17,091
Interest Earned: ₹1,17,091

How to Use

Enter principal • Interest rate • Tenure • Choose compounding frequency

FAQ

Q. Is FD interest taxable?
Yes, added to your income and taxed as per slab.

Q. What is FD laddering?
Splitting money into multiple FDs with different tenures.
