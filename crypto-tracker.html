What these numbers mean

Market cap is price multiplied by total circulating supply — it's why a coin with a tiny price can still be "bigger" than one priced in the thousands, if enough of it exists. 24h volume shows how much was actually traded in the last day — high volume relative to market cap generally means a more liquid, easily tradeable asset, while low volume can mean wider price swings on smaller trades.

A word on accuracy and timing

This pulls aggregated market data, refreshed roughly every 1-5 minutes at the source and re-fetched here every 60 seconds — close to real-time for general awareness, but not the live order-book feed an exchange itself would show you. Treat the percentages and prices here as "where things roughly stand," not as the exact price you'd execute a trade at.

Frequently Asked Questions

How often do prices update?
The tracker refreshes automatically every 60 seconds. The underlying data source itself typically updates every 1-5 minutes, so refreshing more often than that wouldn't show newer information anyway.
Is this real-time trading data?
No — treat it as a close approximation for general awareness, not as a live trading terminal. For actual trades, use your exchange's own live order book.
Where does the data come from?
Aggregated market data from CoinGecko, a widely used cryptocurrency data provider, fetched directly by your browser.
Is my data or activity tracked?
No personal data is collected by this tool. Your browser fetches public price data directly; nothing about you is sent anywhere.
Is this investment advice?
No. This is informational market data only. Cryptocurrency is highly volatile — do your own research and consider consulting a financial advisor before investing.
