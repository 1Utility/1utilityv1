💧 Water Intake Calculator

Frequently Asked Questions

Does this include water from food and other drinks?
No — this estimates water/fluid intake specifically. Food typically contributes another 20% or so of total hydration on top of this figure in a normal diet.
Is more water always better?
No — significantly overdrinking water can dilute sodium levels (hyponatremia) in rare cases, especially among endurance athletes. This target is a sensible range, not a minimum to exceed aggressively.
Why does activity level matter so much?
Sweat loss during exercise can easily exceed 1 liter per hour in hot conditions, which is why active or hot-climate adjustments add meaningfully more to the daily target than sedentary indoor conditions.
