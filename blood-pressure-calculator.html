Frequently Asked Questions

Is a single reading enough to diagnose hypertension?
No — blood pressure naturally fluctuates throughout the day. A diagnosis typically requires multiple elevated readings across separate visits, not a one-off measurement.
What should I do if my reading falls in the 'Crisis' category?
A reading above 180/120 mmHg, especially with symptoms like chest pain, shortness of breath, or severe headache, warrants immediate medical attention rather than waiting for a routine appointment.
Can stress or caffeine before measuring affect the result?
Yes — caffeine, stress, exercise, and even a full bladder can temporarily raise readings, which is why clinics recommend sitting quietly for several minutes before measuring.
