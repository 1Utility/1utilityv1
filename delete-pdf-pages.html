How it works

Click any page thumbnail to mark it for removal — a red checkmark appears on marked pages. Everything you don't mark stays in the output file, in its original order. This is the mirror image of Extract Pages: there you pick what to keep, here you pick what to remove.

Common reasons to delete pages

Removing a blank scanned page, cutting a cover sheet before forwarding a document, or trimming an outdated section from a report before resharing it are the most common cases.

Frequently Asked Questions

Can I delete every page?
The tool requires at least one page to remain, since a zero-page PDF isn't a valid file — you can't select all pages for deletion.
Does this modify my original file?
No — your original PDF is untouched. A new PDF is created with the selected pages removed.
What if I mark the wrong page?
Click it again before running Delete to deselect it — nothing is finalized until you click the Delete button.
Is there a page count limit?
No limit is enforced; very large PDFs may take a few seconds longer to render thumbnails.
Is my file uploaded anywhere?
No — this runs entirely in your browser using JavaScript. Nothing is sent to a server.
