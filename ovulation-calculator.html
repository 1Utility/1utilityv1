Frequently Asked Questions

How accurate is this for irregular cycles?
Less accurate the more irregular your cycle is — this method assumes a fairly consistent luteal phase. Ovulation predictor kits or tracking basal body temperature give more precise real-time confirmation.
Why is the fertile window several days, not just one day?
Sperm can survive in the reproductive tract for up to 5 days, while the egg is viable for about 24 hours after release — so the days leading up to and including ovulation are all potentially fertile.
Can stress or illness shift ovulation timing?
Yes — stress, illness, travel, and significant weight changes can all delay or shift ovulation, which is part of why predictions are estimates rather than guarantees.
