Frequently Asked Questions

Why does the same score give different grades across systems?
Each grading convention sets its own percentage bands and labels — there's no universal standard, so the same 78% genuinely means something different depending on which system is grading it.
Which system should I use for my school?
Use whichever convention your institution actually follows — check your syllabus or report card format if unsure, since Indian boards, US schools, and UK universities all differ.
Is UK '2:1' the same as a percentage band?
Yes, in this tool — UK university classifications (First, 2:1, 2:2, Third) are mapped from percentage bands, mirroring how most UK universities classify undergraduate degree results.
