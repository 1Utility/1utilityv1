Frequently Asked Questions

Should I use gross or in-hand salary here?
Either works as long as both old and new figures use the same basis — comparing gross-to-gross or in-hand-to-in-hand gives a meaningful percentage; mixing the two doesn't.
How is this different from the Salary Hike Calculator?
This works from two monthly salary figures directly; the Salary Hike Calculator starts from an annual CTC and a hike percentage. Use whichever matches the numbers you actually have.
Does this account for tax changes between old and new salary?
No — it's a straightforward arithmetic comparison of the two figures you enter, without adjusting for any tax-bracket shift the increase might cause.
