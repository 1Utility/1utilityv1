Frequently Asked Questions

Why is my actual bonus lower than the percentage quoted?
The percentage quoted in your offer or appraisal letter is the gross bonus before tax — like regular salary, it's taxed at your applicable slab rate before it reaches your account.
Which tax slab should I select?
Use the marginal tax rate that applies to your total annual income including the bonus — if you're unsure, your last payslip's TDS deduction is a reasonable guide.
Is bonus taxed differently from regular salary?
No — bonuses are added to your total income for the year and taxed at the same slab rates as regular salary; there's no separate 'bonus tax rate' in India.
