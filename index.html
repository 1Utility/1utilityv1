Frequently Asked Questions

Is 1Utility actually free?
Yes. Every calculator on this site is free, with no usage limits, paywalls, or sign-up requirement. The site is supported by display advertising rather than subscriptions.
Where does my calculation data go?
Nowhere off your device. Every EMI, SIP, BMI, or salary figure you enter is computed directly in your browser with JavaScript — none of it is sent to a server or stored by us.
How current are the tax slabs and interest rate examples?
Indian income-tax calculators follow the published old-regime and new-regime slabs for the current financial year, including the standard deduction and Section 87A rebate. Interest-rate comparison tables are illustrative ranges, not live bank rates — always confirm the exact rate with your lender before signing.
Which currency do the finance calculators use?
The site auto-detects your region from your browser's timezone and defaults to your local currency symbol, with 20+ currencies available from the region selector. The currency converter itself pulls live mid-market exchange rates.
Can I rely on the health calculators instead of seeing a doctor?
No. BMI, BMR, blood pressure category, and diabetes-risk results are educational screening estimates based on standard published formulas — they're a starting point for a conversation with a healthcare professional, not a diagnosis.
Do these calculators work outside India?
The Health and Academic tools work for anyone. The Salary & Tax tools are built around Indian payroll structures (PF, gratuity, HRA, old/new tax regime), though the Income Tax calculator also supports US and UK brackets.
