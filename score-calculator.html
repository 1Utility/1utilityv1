Frequently Asked Questions

Why does negative marking matter for guessing strategy?
If the penalty for a wrong answer is smaller than the expected value of a random guess among the remaining options, blind guessing has positive expected value; if larger, it's better to leave the question blank — the exact math depends on the number of options and penalty size.
Does this calculator know my specific exam's penalty?
No — enter the exact penalty per wrong answer for your specific exam (check the official exam pattern document), since this varies significantly between exams.
Is 'raw score' before or after negative marking is applied?
Raw score here should be your score before any negative marking is applied — i.e., marks for correct answers only — and the calculator then subtracts the penalty for wrong answers on top.
