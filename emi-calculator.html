EMI Formula

EMI = [P × r × (1+r)^n] / [(1+r)^n - 1]

P = Principal Loan Amount
r = Monthly Interest Rate
n = Total Number of Months

Example

Loan Amount: ₹50,00,000
Interest Rate: 8.5%
Tenure: 20 Years

Monthly EMI: ₹43,391
Total Interest: ₹54,13,843

How to Use

1. Enter your loan amount
2. Enter the interest rate offered by the bank
3. Select loan tenure in years
4. Click on "Calculate EMI"

Comparison

Home Loan: 8–9.5%
Car Loan: 9.5–12%
Personal Loan: 12–24%

FAQ

Q. What is EMI?
Equated Monthly Installment – fixed monthly payment towards loan.

Q. Does longer tenure reduce EMI?
Yes, but you pay more interest overall.

Q. Can I prepay the loan?
Yes, most banks allow prepayment which reduces total interest.
