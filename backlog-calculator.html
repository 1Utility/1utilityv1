Frequently Asked Questions

Will clearing a backlog always raise my CGPA?
Only if the grade you earn on the backlog is higher than your current CGPA — clearing it with a lower grade than your current average will actually pull your CGPA down slightly, not up.
Does a backlog stay on my transcript even after clearing it?
Policies vary by university — some show only the latest grade, others retain a record that the subject was a backlog/re-attempt. Check your institution's specific transcript policy.
Why does credit weight matter for the impact size?
A backlog subject with more credits has proportionally more influence on your overall CGPA once factored in, compared to a low-credit backlog, since CGPA is a credit-weighted average.
