Frequently Asked Questions

Does this account for US state income tax?
No — this covers US federal income tax only. State tax varies widely (some states have none) and would need to be calculated separately for your specific state.
Is the UK calculation England-specific?
Yes — it uses England/Wales/Northern Ireland rates; Scotland has its own separate income tax bands that differ from the rest of the UK.
Which India regime should I select?
Select New Regime if you don't have significant deductions to claim; Old Regime if you have substantial 80C, HRA, or other deductions — compare both for your actual numbers using the dedicated Old/New Tax calculators for more detail.
