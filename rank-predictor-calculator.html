Frequently Asked Questions

How reliable is this compared to official rank predictors?
Official predictors (run by coaching institutes) use actual historical score-vs-rank data from previous years' results, making them considerably more accurate than this simplified percentile-based estimate.
Why does candidate pool size matter so much?
The same percentile score corresponds to a very different absolute rank depending on how many people took the exam — a 95th percentile score means a very different rank number among 10,000 candidates vs 1,000,000.
Should I rely on this for college admission planning?
Use official rank predictors and previous years' cutoff data from the exam body for actual decisions — this tool is a quick estimate only.
