Frequently Asked Questions

How accurate is the LMP method?
It's a solid first estimate, but only about 4% of babies arrive on the exact calculated date — a first-trimester ultrasound dating scan is more precise and is what most doctors rely on after the initial estimate.
What if I have irregular cycles?
The cycle-length adjustment helps, but very irregular cycles make LMP dating less reliable — an early ultrasound is the better option in that case.
What do the three trimesters mean?
Roughly weeks 1–13 (1st), 14–27 (2nd), and 28–40+ (3rd) — each associated with different stages of fetal development and different routine prenatal checks.
