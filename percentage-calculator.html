Frequently Asked Questions

How is percentage different from CGPA?
Percentage is a direct ratio of marks to total marks. CGPA is a weighted grade-point average on a 10-point (or other) scale — converting between them requires a multiplier specific to your university, not a fixed universal formula.
What's considered a good percentage in India?
Conventions vary by board and stream, but broadly: 90%+ is often labelled outstanding/distinction, 75-89% first class, 60-74% second class, and below that varies by institution's pass criteria.
Can I use this for aggregate marks across multiple subjects?
Yes — add up marks obtained across all subjects for the numerator, and the sum of maximum marks for each subject as the total.
