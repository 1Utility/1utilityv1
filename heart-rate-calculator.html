Frequently Asked Questions

How accurate is 220 minus age for max heart rate?
It's a population average with meaningful individual variation (±10-15 bpm is common) — a supervised max-effort test gives a more precise number if you need it for serious training.
Why does resting heart rate matter for zone calculation?
The Karvonen method scales zones to your actual heart-rate reserve (max minus resting), which better reflects individual fitness than zones based purely on a percentage of max HR.
Which zone should I train in most?
Most endurance training guidance favors spending the bulk of training time in Zone 2 (aerobic/fat-burn range), with smaller amounts of higher-intensity work — but goals vary by sport and individual program.
