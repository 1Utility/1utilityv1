Retirement Formula

Future Expenses = Current Expenses × (1 + Inflation)^Years

Corpus Needed = Future Monthly Expense × 12 × Annuity Factor

Example

Age 30 → Retire at 60
Current Expenses: ₹50,000/month

FAQ

Q. How much corpus do I need?
Generally 25–30 times your annual expenses.
