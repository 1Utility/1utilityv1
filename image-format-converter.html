Choosing the right format

JPG is best for photos — small file sizes, no transparency support, some quality loss from compression. PNG is best for graphics, screenshots, and anything needing transparency — larger files, no quality loss. WEBP is a modern format offering smaller file sizes than JPG or PNG at similar quality, with wide browser support — a good general-purpose choice for web use.

About the quality setting

The quality slider only affects JPG and WEBP output, since PNG uses lossless compression with no quality parameter — converting to PNG always preserves full image data regardless of that setting.

Frequently Asked Questions

Does converting PNG to JPG lose transparency?
Yes — JPG doesn't support transparency at all. Any transparent areas in your original PNG will be filled with white (or another solid background) in the JPG output.
What's the best format for web use?
WEBP typically offers the smallest file size at comparable visual quality and is supported by all modern browsers, making it a strong general default for websites.
Will converting to PNG make my file bigger?
Often yes, especially for photos — PNG's lossless compression generally produces larger files than JPG or WEBP for photographic content, though it's ideal for graphics and screenshots.
Is my image uploaded anywhere?
No — conversion happens entirely in your browser using the Canvas API. Nothing is sent to a server.
