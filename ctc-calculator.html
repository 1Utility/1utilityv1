Frequently Asked Questions

Why do two offers with the same CTC feel so different in hand?
Because the split between fixed and variable pay, and the basic-to-allowances ratio, can vary a lot between companies even at an identical CTC — a higher fixed-pay share generally means more predictable take-home.
Is employer PF contribution money I get to keep?
Yes, eventually — it accumulates in your EPF account and is yours, but it's locked away for retirement/specific withdrawal conditions, not available as monthly cash.
Should I negotiate based on CTC or fixed pay?
Fixed pay is the more reliable number to negotiate and compare across offers, since variable pay depends on performance ratings and company performance that you can't fully control.
