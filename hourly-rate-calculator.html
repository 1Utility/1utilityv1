Frequently Asked Questions

Is this a fair comparison against a freelance/contract hourly rate?
It's a useful starting comparison, but remember a CTC figure usually includes benefits (PF, insurance, paid leave) that a pure hourly contract rate doesn't — a contractor often needs a meaningfully higher hourly rate to match the same effective value.
Why does 'working weeks per year' default to 50, not 52?
It accounts for typical annual leave/holidays — 52 weeks minus roughly 2 weeks of time off is a common approximation, though you can adjust this to your actual leave entitlement.
Should I use gross CTC or take-home for this calculation?
Use whichever basis you want the resulting hourly rate to represent — gross CTC gives a pre-tax hourly equivalent, while using your actual take-home gives a post-tax hourly figure.
