Frequently Asked Questions

Why might this differ slightly from another age calculator?
Most discrepancies come from how leap years and month-length differences are handled. This tool counts actual calendar years/months/days rather than approximating with a flat 365-day year.
Can I calculate age as of a future or past date, not just today?
Yes — change the "Calculate As Of" field to any date to find age at that specific point, useful for eligibility cutoffs or historical records.
Can I use this for legal or official age verification?
Use it for personal reference only — official documents and forms should rely on government-issued ID and the exact rules of whichever institution is asking.
