How to rotate

Use the two buttons to rotate every page in the document 90° clockwise or counter-clockwise in one step. To fix just one or two problem pages instead, click directly on that page's thumbnail to rotate only it — each click turns that page another 90°.

Frequently Asked Questions

Can I rotate just one page instead of the whole document?
Yes — click an individual page thumbnail to rotate only that page, independent of the others.
What if a page needs a full 180° turn?
Click it twice (or use the all-pages button twice) — each click rotates 90°, so two clicks gets you to 180°.
Does rotating affect page content or quality?
No — rotation only changes each page's display orientation, the underlying content is untouched.
Is my file uploaded anywhere?
No — rotation happens entirely in your browser using JavaScript. Nothing is sent to a server.
