Investing beyond ₹1.5 lakh across these instruments combined doesn't earn any extra tax deduction — the cap is on the total, not per instrument. Once you've filled the ₹1.5 lakh limit, additional 80C-eligible investments still have their own merits (returns, lock-in, safety) but no further tax benefit under this specific section.

Frequently Asked Questions

Is the ₹1.5 lakh limit per instrument or total?
Total — it's a combined cap across all Section 80C investments (ELSS, PPF, LIC, home loan principal, NSC, tax-saving FDs, etc.), not ₹1.5 lakh for each individually.
Is Section 80C available under the New Tax Regime?
No — Section 80C deductions are only available under the Old Tax Regime. If you've opted for the New Regime, this deduction doesn't apply to your tax calculation.
Which 80C instrument gives the best return?
ELSS funds have historically offered the highest return potential among 80C options (with market risk and a 3-year lock-in), while PPF offers guaranteed, tax-free returns with a much longer lock-in — the right choice depends on your risk appetite and time horizon.
