Frequently Asked Questions

Which divisor should I use?
Use whichever multiplier your specific university applies in its official CGPA-to-percentage formula — ×9.5 is common, but always confirm with your institution's exam cell for anything submitted formally.
Is this exactly the inverse of the CGPA-to-percentage formula?
Yes — dividing by the same multiplier used to convert CGPA to percentage gets you back to an equivalent CGPA, assuming a linear relationship, which is what most universities use.
Can I get a CGPA above 10 from this?
No — if your percentage divided by the multiplier exceeds 10, that typically indicates either an unusually high percentage or a multiplier mismatch; CGPA on this scale caps at 10.
