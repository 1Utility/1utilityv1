Frequently Asked Questions

Is the New Regime automatically applied?
Yes — it's the default regime since FY 2023-24. Salaried taxpayers who prefer the Old Regime (to claim HRA, 80C, etc.) need to explicitly opt in each year.
What is the Section 87A rebate?
If your taxable income after the standard deduction is ₹7 lakh or below, the rebate brings your tax liability to zero under the New Regime — effectively a full tax waiver up to that threshold.
Can I claim HRA or 80C under the New Regime?
Almost no exemptions or deductions are available under the New Regime — that's the trade-off for its lower rates. The standard deduction is one of the few exceptions still allowed.
