Frequently Asked Questions

Why is my total hike percentage higher than the base hike I was told?
Because promotions often come with a higher variable/bonus percentage on top of the base hike — the combined effect on total package looks larger than the base salary increase alone.
Should I treat the bonus portion as guaranteed income?
No — variable pay is typically linked to individual and company performance ratings, so budgeting as if 100% of it is guaranteed is risky; treat the base/fixed pay figure as your reliable number.
Does a promotion always come with a bigger hike than a regular appraisal?
Generally yes, since it reflects a role and responsibility change, but the actual percentage varies enormously by company, industry, and how significant the role change is.
