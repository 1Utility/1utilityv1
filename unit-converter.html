Frequently Asked Questions

Why is temperature handled differently from the other categories?
Length, weight, volume, speed, and area all convert by a fixed multiplication factor. Temperature scales have different starting points (0°C ≠ 0°F ≠ 0K), so converting requires an offset calculation, not just a multiplier.
How precise are the conversion factors?
Conversion factors are accurate to at least 6 significant figures, which is precise enough for everyday, academic, and most professional use.
Can I convert between categories, like weight to volume?
No — categories like weight and volume measure fundamentally different physical properties (mass vs space) and aren't interchangeable without knowing the substance's density.
