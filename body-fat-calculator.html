Frequently Asked Questions

How accurate is the tape-measure method vs DEXA scans?
It's typically within 3-4% of more precise methods like DEXA or hydrostatic weighing for most body types, making it a solid free estimate rather than a clinical-grade measurement.
Why does the women's formula need a hip measurement too?
Female fat distribution patterns differ enough from male patterns that the US Navy formula adds a hip measurement to its equation specifically for women, improving accuracy.
What's a healthy body fat range?
Roughly 14-24% for men and 21-31% for women is considered the 'average to fitness' healthy band, though athletes often sit lower and some healthy ranges extend slightly wider by age.
