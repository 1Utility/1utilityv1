Frequently Asked Questions

Can this calculator diagnose diabetes?
No — it's a risk-screening estimate based on known contributing factors. Diagnosis requires an actual blood test (fasting glucose, HbA1c, or oral glucose tolerance test) ordered by a doctor.
What should I do if my score comes back High or Very High Risk?
Treat it as a prompt to get an actual blood glucose or HbA1c test from a healthcare provider — early detection and lifestyle changes are far more effective before a diagnosis than after.
Why does BMI factor into diabetes risk?
Excess body fat, particularly visceral fat, is strongly linked to insulin resistance — one of the core mechanisms behind Type 2 diabetes development.
